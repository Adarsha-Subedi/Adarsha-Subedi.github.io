About me

I am an economics researcher interested in how households make decisions and how economic institutions and public policy shape people's opportunities.

My interests include migration and remittances, education and human capital, public economics, and industrial organization. I am interested in both the theoretical mechanisms behind economic behavior and empirical work that helps us understand real-world outcomes.

This website brings together information about my research interests, projects, teaching, and academic work. I will update it as papers and materials become available.

Research

My research interests span development and applied microeconomics, with particular attention to the choices made by households and the incentives created by policy.

  • Migration and remittances. Household decisions, resource allocation, and economic outcomes in communities of origin.
  • Education and human capital. Investment in skills and schooling, constraints on household choices, and long-run outcomes.
  • Public economics. Policy design, incentives, and the effects of public programs on behavior and welfare.
  • Industrial organization. Market structure, strategic interaction, and the role of information in markets.
