01 / About

Curiosity, guided
by evidence.

I am an economics researcher with interests in applied and theoretical questions about development, household decisions, and public policy.

My current interests include the links between migration and remittances, investments in education and human capital, and the incentives created by economic institutions. I am also interested in public finance and industrial organization.

This site is a home for my research, working papers, teaching materials, and academic updates. It will grow as projects develop.
