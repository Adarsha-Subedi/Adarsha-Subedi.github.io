My research interests span development and applied microeconomics, with particular attention to household decisions and the incentives created by policy.

## Research interests

- **Migration and remittances:** Household decisions, resource allocation, and economic outcomes in communities of origin.
- **Education and human capital:** Investment in skills and schooling, constraints on household choices, and long-run outcomes.
- **Public economics:** Policy design, incentives, and the effects of public programs on behavior and welfare.
- **Industrial organization:** Market structure, strategic interaction, and the role of information in markets.
